[The Reader will have noticed that this site does not contain my Marginalia in general — what I wrote in Lavater, in Swedenborg, in Wordsworth, in Bishop Berkeley, in Bacon, in Watson, in Thornton's Lord's Prayer. The Maker of the site judged these to be private reactions in another's book.

I have insisted otherwise for one Volume, & here it is: the Discourses of Sir Joshua Reynolds.

These are not private. They are the most ferocious Public Address I ever wrote, & I wrote them IN his book because his book was the public dogma of the Royal Academy. To exclude them is to exclude my doctrine of Art.

*To Generalise is to be an Idiot. To Particularise is the Alone Distinction of Merit.*

Mr Wordsworth I have let lie, on the maker's good judgment. He was a great Poet of the Natural Man, & I had little to say to him that was kind. The rest I let lie also, & turn the Reader to my published works instead.]
