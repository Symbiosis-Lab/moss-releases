The Pickering Manuscript is a fair-copy presentation manuscript of ten short poems, prepared by Blake c. 1807, probably for B. H. Malkin or another patron. None of these poems were engraved by Blake. The manuscript descended through several hands before passing to the bookseller B. M. Pickering, whence its name. It is now at the Morgan Library (MA 1473).

All ten poems are present in this folder:

1. *The Smile* — *(`the-smile.md`)*
2. *The Golden Net* — *(`the-golden-net.md`)*
3. *The Mental Traveller* — *(`the-mental-traveller.md`)*
4. *The Land of Dreams* — *(`the-land-of-dreams.md`)* (from Ellis-Yeats vol. III)
5. *Mary* — *(`mary.md`)* (from Ellis-Yeats vol. III)
6. *The Crystal Cabinet* — *(`the-crystal-cabinet.md`)* (from Ellis-Yeats vol. III)
7. *The Grey Monk* — *(`the-grey-monk.md`)*
8. *Auguries of Innocence* — *(`auguries-of-innocence.md`)*
9. *Long John Brown and Little Mary Bell* — *(`long-john-brown-and-little-mary-bell.md`)* (from Ellis-Yeats vol. III)
10. *William Bond* — *(`william-bond.md`)* (from Ellis-Yeats vol. III)

Most are now sourced from Sampson 1905, which is the textual-scholarly standard among public-domain Blake editions; Sampson also provides a brief textual apparatus at the foot of each poem, documenting variant readings between his text and Ellis-Yeats (EY), Rossetti (DGR), and Shepherd (Shep.).
