A small Manuscript of finished short Poems, written out in fair copy about the year seven, probably for Mr B. H. Malkin or another patron. I did not engrave these. The papers came down to a Mr B. M. Pickering long after my death — hence the Manuscript's name.

Contained:

- **Auguries of Innocence** — *To see a World in a Grain of Sand, And a Heaven in a Wild Flower...*
- **The Mental Traveller** — *I travel'd thro' a Land of Men, A Land of Men & Women too...*
- **The Crystal Cabinet** — *The Maiden caught me in the Wild...*
- **The Grey Monk** — *I die, I die! the Mother said...*
- **Mary** — *Sweet Mary, the first time she ever was there...*
- **The Smile** — *There is a Smile of Love...*
- **The Golden Net** — *Three Virgins at the break of day...*
- **The Land of Dreams** — *Awake, awake, my little Boy! Thou wast thy Mother's only joy...*
- **Long John Brown & Little Mary Bell**
- **William Bond**

*Full transcriptions to be drawn from Sampson 1905 (PD).*
