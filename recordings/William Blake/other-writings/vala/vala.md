source: Manuscript at British Library. Text via Ellis & Yeats, *Works of William Blake* vol. III (Bernard Quaritch, 1893). Public domain. Note: Ellis's *Vala* text is the most interventionist of any 19th-century printing; he silently emended Blake's manuscript at numerous points. Modern critical editions (Bentley 1963, Erdman 1988) give substantially different texts. The present site transmits Ellis-Yeats with that caveat.

Blake worked on *Vala* from c. 1796 through 1807, accumulating multiple states of revision on the same sheets. He never engraved it. Much of its matter was later transposed, with substantial change, into *Milton* and *Jerusalem*.
