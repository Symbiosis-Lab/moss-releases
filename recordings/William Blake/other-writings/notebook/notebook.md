The Notebook is a small folio I bought used; it had been my Brother Robert's. From his death in eighty-seven I kept it as my drawing-book & rough-book, & I went on writing & drawing in it for forty years. Some pages are upside-down, some are written across other writing. Mr Rossetti, who came by it years after I was gone, will catalogue what is there.

I shall not put these on copper. They are for whoever finds them after me.

Contained here (selections, with sources):

- **The Everlasting Gospel** — long verse fragment, c. 1818, on the question of the true Jesus.
- **The Vision of the Last Judgment** — prose, c. 1810. *(See Erdman ed., pp. 554–566; this folder will hold the transcript when extracted from primary source.)*
- **Public Address** — prose, c. 1810, on engraving, the Knaves of the Trade, the Cromek/Stothard affair, & the case for my Canterbury Pilgrims. *(See Erdman pp. 571–582.)*
- **Selected Epigrams** — "Mock on, Mock on Voltaire Rousseau"; "I asked a thief to steal me a Peach"; the Hayley & Schiavonetti epigrams; "When Klopstock England defied"; etc.
