A long verse fragment drafted by Blake in the Notebook (Rossetti Manuscript, now BL Add. MS 49460) in the years after the 1809 Exhibition, datable to c. 1818. Never engraved. The text contests the orthodox image of Christ as meek and submissive, asserting an alternative vision.

The opening lines:

> The Vision of Christ that thou dost see
> Is my Vision's Greatest Enemy.

For the full extracted text (Ellis & Yeats 1893 transcription), see *everlasting-gospel-text.md* in this folder (pending separation from the editorial commentary surrounding it in vol. II).
