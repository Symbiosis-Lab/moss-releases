In the year ninety-one I wrote a long Poem in seven Books on the Revolution in France, the first of which Mr Joseph Johnson set in type at his shop in St Paul's Churchyard. The proof-sheets were drawn off & the title-page printed.

But the political climate had sharpen'd. The warrant against Paine had gone out the year before. Mr Johnson's courage flagged at the last & he did not put it forth. The remaining six Books were never written.

The proof-copy survives. Only the first Book was completed; it is what follows.

*Title page text:*

> THE FRENCH REVOLUTION
> A POEM, IN SEVEN BOOKS.
>
> BOOK THE FIRST.
>
> LONDON: PRINTED FOR J. JOHNSON,
> No. 72, St. Paul's Church-yard. MDCCXCI.
> [PRICE ONE SHILLING.]
>
> *Advertisement:* The remaining Books of this Poem are finished, and will be published in their Order.

*(The publishing claim "remaining Books are finished" was Mr Johnson's hopeful frontispiece, not a true statement. The remaining Books were not finished. They never were.)*
