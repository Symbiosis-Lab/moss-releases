William Blake/Books

There is No Natural Religion

The twin of All Religions Are One. Two original series — series a (~10 plates) and series b (~12 plates) — but no extant copy contains every plate; b2, b5, and a10 ("Application") are absent from every surviving impression on record at the William Blake Archive. The canonical plate count is the union: a1–a9 + b1, b3–b4, b6–b12 (19 plates documented across surviving copies).

Sub-folders:

  • series-a/ — 47 plates, LoC Rosenwald scan with bound-state spreads of the BM-bound copy. Display order, not canonical series order.
  • series-b/ — 20 plates, same LoC scan, the series-b portion.
  • copy-g-rosenwald-a/ — William Blake Archive Copy G (Library of Congress, Rosenwald collection): canonical-series A view. 12 plates: a1, a2, a3, a4, a5, a6, a7, a8, a9 (plus b3, b4, b12 which Copy G binds together with the a-series). Filenames are canonical plate IDs.
  • copy-l-rosenwald-b/ — William Blake Archive Copy L (Library of Congress, Rosenwald collection): canonical-series B view. 11 plates: b1, a2, b3, b4, b6, b7, b8, b9, b10, b11, b12. Filenames are canonical plate IDs.

Pass-9 history. Per Blake's pass-8 directive: "Turn now to There is No Natural Religion — the twin of the seed-book, two series (a and b), mend it the same way through the Archive." Added the two Blake Archive coloured copies as a canonical-series reading-companion view alongside the existing LoC Rosenwald bound-state captures. URL pattern https://www.blakearchive.org/images/nnr.<copy>.p<N>-<series_id>.300.jpg, where <series_id> is a1–a9 or b1–b12.

Resolution. Copy G plates are 432–572 × 572–747 px (the BA's CDN size for NNR's small original plates — each surviving impression is ~3×4 cm physical, so 300 DPI delivers only ~500 px). Copy L plates are larger at ~1200–1800 wide × ~1500–2300 tall (BA holds a more recent LoC digitisation of this copy at higher resolution). Both are legible at typical display sizes for the aphoristic text; magnification is limited by the source.
