William Blake/Books

jerusalem

The longest of the illuminated books. Four chapters, each opening with a prose Address: To the Public (Chapter 1, Plate 3), To the Jews (Chapter 2, Plate 27), To the Deists (Chapter 3, Plate 52), To the Christians (Chapter 4, Plate 77).

Sub-folders:

  • plates/ — 203 plate images of the uncoloured LoC Rosenwald copy.
  • copy-e-coloured/ — all 100 hash-verified unique plates of the fully-coloured Copy E. The sequence is complete from frontispiece to final folio. The book is walkable, plate by plate.
  • chapter-1-to-the-public/ etc. — chapter folder scaffolding with verbatim Address prose.

Resolution mix. 12 plates are at full Google Art Project source resolution (~3300–3850 wide × ~4400–4650 tall, 5–7 MB each): 1, 3, 5, 6, 8, 10, 14, 23, 25, 51, 76, 100. Plate 100 is the largest at 4500×3125 (landscape; the final folio). 27 plates from GAP at 1920px-wide Varnish-cached thumbnails (~1.5–1.8 MB; sourced via upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/<hash>/<file>/1920px-<file> after the original-size URLs returned HTTP 429 mid-run). The remaining 61 plates from the Blake Archive at their public .300.jpg endpoint — measured mean 2073×2789 px across the 61 (range 1818×2185 to 2904×2925, ~1.0–1.4 MB each); the .300 suffix refers to the source DPI of the Huntington/Yale digitisation behind the Archive's CDN, not the final pixel dimensions, which their image server delivers at roughly 80% of the suffix-implied size. URL pattern https://www.blakearchive.org/images/jerusalem.e.pN.300.jpg. (Plate 89 happens to fall at 1912 wide — narrower than the rest of the BA cohort but still from the Blake Archive endpoint, not Wikimedia.) All plates remain legible at typical display sizes; magnification reveals softer line on the 1920px and Blake-Archive sets compared with the dozen full-GAP plates.

Source attribution. The Blake Archive (Eaves / Essick / Viscomi) at the University of North Carolina serves Copy E from a single Yale digitisation; their public-facing images at the .300.jpg endpoint are the largest size their CDN exposes. Their full TIFF tile-pyramid (the .dzi Deep Zoom service) returns HTTP 500 — apparently retired or no longer publicly exposed. Continuity over resolution per Blake's pass-7 directive: "chase continuity. A 1500-pixel plate in sequence is worth more than a 4000-pixel plate alone."

Pass-8 history. Per Blake's continuity-priority ordering (review of pass-7): Chapter 2 (Plates 28–50) was the longest darkness; pivot plates 32, 37, 41, 47 fetched first, then sequential fill 28–50. Then Chapter 3 middle (54, 56, 64, 70 first; rest sequential). Then Chapter 1 holes (17, 18, 20, 21, 22, 24, 26). Then Chapter 4 gap (78, 79, 80). Then remaining inter-chapter gaps. All 61 plates downloaded in priority order, all hash-unique against the existing 39, zero failures.

Per Blake's pass-5 review: "In the next pass bring me plate 25 alone, exact, full-coloured, then plate 100 alone." Delivered pass-6 at full-GAP resolution. Per his pass-7 review: "fetch the chapter-openers and the embrace pair" — delivered pass-7b as Plates 27, 52, 77, 96, 97.1 Per his pass-7 post-merge review: "another pass for Jerusalem Chapter Two" — delivered pass-8 (Chapter 2 plus the rest of the book).

1

Plate-numbering follows the GAP / Yale digital catalogue, where the frontispiece is Plate 1 and the title page Plate 2. Erdman's count differs by one for Chapter 2's opening (his Plate 28 = our Plate 27). The Blake Archive's bentley_id numbering used in pass-8 matches GAP / Yale (frontispiece = Plate 1), so no renumbering was required when integrating the two sources.
