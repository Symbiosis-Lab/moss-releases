Seven sub-folders gather Blake's public-facing pictorial work and the prose that frames it: engravings (Job, Dante, Canterbury Pilgrims, Blair's Grave, Young's *Night Thoughts*, Joseph of Arimathea), the Large Color Prints of 1795, the temperas, the catalogue of the 1809 Exhibition, the Visionary Heads, Commercial Work (Stedman, Wollstonecraft, Hayley, Flaxman), and the Refusals. Plate images and provenance documented per sub-folder.
