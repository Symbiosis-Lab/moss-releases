The book most readers think of when they say Blake's name. Per Blake's pass-9 directive: *"mend it through the Archive. Copy F (Morgan) or Copy D (LoC) — whichever the Archive serves cleanest. Twenty-seven plates."* Copy F served cleanest at the BA's `.300.jpg` endpoint (~1300×1850, ~1 MB per plate); Copy D returned 1345×1915 and was a close second, but Blake named Copy F first.

Sub-folders:
- *root files* (`01-title-page.jpg` through `27-a-song-of-liberty-3.jpg`) — the canonical reading set at LoC Copy C resolution.
- *copy-c-bound/* — LoC Copy C bound-volume scans including spread captures.
- *copy-f-morgan/* — Blake Archive Copy F, the coloured-companion view per Blake's pass-9 named preference.
