Also etched intaglio, like *Ahania*, & also surviving in only one complete copy. The Eternal Prophet Los, in chains of the mind, beating the stubborn structure of his fallen Brother.

> O Times remote!
> When Love & Joy were adoration,
> And none impure were deem'd:
> Not Eyeless Covet,
> Nor Thin-lip'd Envy,
> Nor Bristled Wrath,
> Nor Curled Wantonness.

The smith of Eternity at his work. The hammer that beats out the vegetable Body of the fallen Urizen.
