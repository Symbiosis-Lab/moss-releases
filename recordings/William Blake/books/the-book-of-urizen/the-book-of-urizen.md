My Genesis. The fall of Urizen, the eldest of the Eternals, who would be a god apart from his brethren — who would bind the Infinite in his Book of brass.

> Lo, a shadow of horror is risen
> In Eternity! Unknown, unprolific!
> Self-clos'd, all-repelling: what Demon
> Hath form'd this abominable void
> This soul-shudd'ring vacuum?—Some said
> "It is Urizen", But unknown, abstracted
> Brooding secret, the dark power hid.

Urizen falls. Los, the eternal Prophet, sees the fall & weeps. Los & Enitharmon are divided. Orc is born of Enitharmon, the spirit of Revolt, & is chain'd to the mountain by Los his father.

Plate order varies across the copies. This presentation follows Copy G's bound order.
