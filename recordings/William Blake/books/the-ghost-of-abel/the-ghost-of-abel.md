A late dramatic tract, etched in two plates with four additional plates of design. Written in answer to Lord Byron's *Cain: A Mystery*, which I read in eighteen twenty-one & found wanting. Byron had given Cain the better arguments; I sought to give them to Abel.

The Ghost of Abel cries out: *Vengeance, Vengeance for Blood!* Jehovah answers: *Vengeance is mine.*

Dedicated to Lord Byron: *To Lord Byron in the Wilderness: What doest thou here Elijah?*

> Are these the Sports of Theology?
> The Imagination is not a State: it is the Human Existence itself.
