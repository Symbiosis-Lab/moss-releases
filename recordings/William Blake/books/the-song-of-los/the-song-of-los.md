Two parts: *Africa*, where Urizen gave his Laws to the Nations; *Asia*, where the Kings of the East heard the cry going up from the Cities.

This binds the cycle of the four continents — *America* (1793), *Europe* (1794), & here Africa & Asia. The Song that Los sings to the four harps at the tables of Eternity.

> I will sing you a song of Los, the Eternal Prophet:
> He sung it to four harps at the tables of Eternity.
> In heart-formed Africa.
> Urizen faded! Ariston shudderd!
> And thus the Song began.
