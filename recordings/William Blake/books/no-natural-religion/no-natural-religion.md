Two short tracts, that Man's perceptions are not bounded by Organs of Perception. *He who sees the Infinite in all things sees God. He who sees the Ratio only sees himself only.*

The Reasoner — meaning Mr Locke, & Mr Newton, & the Schools — would have it that all Knowledge comes from the Senses. I say: the Senses are the gateway, but the Knowledge is in the Poetic Genius, & precedes the Senses.

*Therefore God becomes as we are, that we may be as he is.*

(Series **a** sets the position of the Reasoner; Series **b** answers him, plate for plate.)
