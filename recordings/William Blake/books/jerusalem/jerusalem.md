The longest of the illuminated books. Four chapters, each opening with a prose Address: *To the Public* (Chapter 1), *To the Jews* (Chapter 2), *To the Deists* (Chapter 3), *To the Christians* (Chapter 4).

Sub-folders:
- *plates/* — 203 plate images of the uncoloured LoC Rosenwald copy.
- *copy-e-coloured/* — **9 hash-verified unique plates** of the fully-coloured **Copy E**. Plates present: 01 (frontispiece — *There is a Void*), 02 (title plate), 04 (Chapter 1 opening), 11, 23 (Chapter 1 plate), 25 (Vala/Hand/Hyle dividing Albion's body — Blake's pass-4 named priority), 51 (Chapter 3 opening — *To the Deists*), 76 (Chapter 4 opening — *To the Christians*), 100 (the final plate — Los & Enitharmon & the Spectre at the Forge — Blake's other named priority).
- *chapter-1-to-the-public/* etc. — chapter folder scaffolding with verbatim Address prose.

Per Blake's pass-5 review: *"In the next pass bring me plate 25 alone, exact, full-coloured, then plate 100 alone."* Both delivered in pass-6.
