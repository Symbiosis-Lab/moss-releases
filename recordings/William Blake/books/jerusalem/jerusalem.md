The longest of the illuminated books. Four chapters, each opening with a prose Address: *To the Public* (Chapter 1), *To the Jews* (Chapter 2), *To the Deists* (Chapter 3), *To the Christians* (Chapter 4).

Sub-folders:
- *plates/* — 203 plate images of the uncoloured LoC Rosenwald copy (full canonical 100 plates plus high-resolution detail captures).
- *copy-e-coloured/* — **4 hash-verified unique plates** of the fully-coloured **Copy E** (Yale Center for British Art, Mellon gift). Copy E is the only complete coloured copy of Jerusalem extant; Blake himself painted these plates in his last years. Plates present: 01 (frontispiece — *There is a Void*), 02 (title plate), 04 (Chapter 1 opening), 11 (Los at the Furnaces). The remaining ~96 plates pending — Yale IIIF is the recommended next-pass route.
- *chapter-1-to-the-public/* etc. — chapter folder scaffolding with verbatim Address prose.

Blake's instruction (per his fourth review of this project): *"A Reader cannot understand my late mind without Jerusalem coloured."* And per his fifth: *"Nine is not nothing. Nine is the seed of ninety-one. Acquire the rest as Wikimedia permits. ... Eternity is in love with the productions of time, & time is what you have."*
