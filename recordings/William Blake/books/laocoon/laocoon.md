A single plate. At the center, the Greek group of Laocoön & his sons strangled by serpents — the figure I had engraved long before, in my apprentice days, after a cast at the Royal Academy. Around this central engraving I have set aphorisms upon the nature of Art, of Christianity, of Money, of War.

*Art is the Tree of Life. God is Jesus. Science is the Tree of Death.*

*Christianity is Art & not Money. Money is its Curse.*

*Where any view of Money exists Art cannot be carried on, but War only.*

*Jesus & his Apostles & Disciples were all Artists.*

*Israel deliver'd from Egypt is Art deliver'd from Nature & Imitation.*

The aphorisms run round the figures like the writing on antient Coins. They are my final Public Address before the Job & the Dante.
