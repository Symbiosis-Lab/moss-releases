Mr John Linnell, a young Painter of Landscapes, was brought by Mr Cumberland to call upon me at South Molton Street. He has been the saving of my last years.

He commission'd from me the *Illustrations of the Book of Job* — twenty-one engravings, & a title plate. He pays me a pound a week against the work, & advances me money when I need it, which is often.

After Job he set me to the *Dante* — one hundred designs for the *Divine Comedy*, & seven of them to be engraved. I shall not live to finish that work; what I leave on the copper Mr Linnell will see thro' the press after me.

He has carried me, & Catherine, thro' these last years. When I move'd to Fountain Court he came to see me three times a week. He brought his children to draw with me. He brought me Samuel Palmer the boy, & George Richmond, & Edward Calvert, & Frederick Tatham. They call themselves *the Ancients*.

I do not know why Mr Linnell took up my cause. I never asked him. Eternity will know.
