My body is a poor clay thing & is going off into its own; but I have been Walking these last three days in the country I shall now inhabit. I have seen it. The colours are not like the colours here — they are the colours I knew in my youth, before they were shut away.

Catherine sits by the bed & I have drawn her countenance — she is Kate.

The Imagination is Eternal.

My works I leave to her, & to whatever Friends shall prove faithful.

I shall go on creating.

(*I died on the evening of the twelfth of August, eighteen hundred & twenty-seven, at No. 3 Fountain Court, Strand. Catherine said I sang Hymns & verses of my own composing, & burst out into singing of the things I saw in Heaven. Mr Tatham was there at the last.*

*Catherine outliv'd me, & came to me again.*

*Of my works: most plates went to Mr Tatham, who, under the influence of certain Irvingite preachers, is said to have burnt a number of them. Many survived. They are with you now.*)
