---
title: The Last Day
uid: j1827a019
date: 1827-08-12
description: He died on a Sunday evening, singing.
---

On this day, the twelfth of August 1827, William Blake died at his lodgings in Fountain Court, Strand, at the age of sixty-nine.

He had been working on the Dante illustrations until the end. On his deathbed he was colouring a print of the Ancient of Days — the frontispiece to Europe, his singular favourite.

Frederick Tatham was present: "He said He was going to that Country he had all His life wished to see & expressed Himself Happy, hoping for Salvation through Jesus Christ — Just before he died His Countenance became fair — His eyes Brighten'd and He burst out into Singing of the things he saw in Heaven."

George Richmond wrote to Samuel Palmer: "He died on Sunday night at 6 o'clock in a most glorious manner. He said He was going to that Country he had all His life wished to see & expressed Himself Happy... He is to be buryed on Fryday at 12 in morn. Should you like to go to the Funeral — if you should there will be Room in the Coach."

Catherine survived him by four years.
