The Bastille is fallen. The Prisoners are free.

They say I wore the *bonnet rouge* in the streets of London this summer — let them say so. The Cap was the badge of the friends of Liberty, & I was a friend of Liberty.

*The tygers of wrath are wiser than the horses of instruction.*

I have begun a long Poem about the Revolution in France, in seven Books, the first of which Mr Johnson will set in type next year — though he will lose his courage at the last & not put it forth.
