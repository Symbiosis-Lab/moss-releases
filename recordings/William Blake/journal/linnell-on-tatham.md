The reception history of Blake's manuscripts after Catherine Blake's death in October 1831 is contested. Frederick Tatham, who had been one of "the Ancients" around Blake from c. 1825, claimed Catherine bequeathed Blake's residual works to him. John Linnell, who knew both parties intimately, directly contested this in his own copy of Gilchrist's *Life*. A. T. Story records the matter in his 1892 *Life of John Linnell*:
