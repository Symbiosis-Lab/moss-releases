We have come back to London this autumn, leaving Mr Hayley's cottage to whomever shall next inhabit it.

The new lodgings are at No. 17 South Molton Street, between Oxford Street & Brook Street. They are small — two rooms on the first floor — but they are cheap & central. Catherine is well.

I am told I am to be tried at Chichester in the January Quarter Sessions. Mr Hayley will come up for me. Mr Rose has agreed to defend, tho' he is ill.

Three years at the seaside, & now I am again in the smoke. I find I had begun to mistake Felpham for Eternity — which it is not. The True Eternity is here, in the streets where Milton walk'd & where Catherine sets the inks.

The Press is uncrated & set up by the parlour window. I begin again.
