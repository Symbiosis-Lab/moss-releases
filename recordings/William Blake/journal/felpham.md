Mr Flaxman wrote to Mr Hayley of Felpham in Sussex, recommending me to engrave for his *Life of Cowper*. Mr Hayley offer'd me a cottage at the bottom of his garden, by the sea, for a low rent.

We have remov'd this day — the eighteenth of September, eighteen-hundred. Catherine, & I, & my sister, & sixteen heavy boxes by waggon from London. The Cottage is small but well-built, of flint & brick, with a parlour & two upper chambers & a thatched roof. The sea is a quarter of a mile off; the smell of salt is in every room.

Mr Hayley received me kindly. He calls me his Gentle Visionary. Catherine is in better health than she has been for three winters.

I shall do well here. I have my press & my coppers & my colours, & the Quiet of the Sea instead of the Noise of the Lambeth streets. Milton descended into my left foot the second day I was here & I saw the heavens open.

I shall write a great Poem about him.
