---
title: The Ancients
uid: j1824a018
date: 1824-01-01
description: A circle of young men who call themselves the Ancients.
---

A circle of young men has formed around me in these last years. They call themselves the Ancients. Samuel Palmer, George Richmond, Edward Calvert, Frederick Tatham — they come to my rooms at Fountain Court and sit at my feet as if I were a prophet returned from the wilderness.

Palmer said, upon first seeing my woodcuts for Virgil: "They are visions of little dells, and nooks, and corners of Paradise." He was so moved he could barely speak.

They are kind. They see what others have not seen. I am sixty-seven years old. I have spent my life in obscurity. And now these young men come and tell me that my work matters.

I believe they are right.
