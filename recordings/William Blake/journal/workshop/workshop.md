Half the copies of the Songs that go out under my name were tinted by Catherine's hand & I knew it & approved it. She set the inks. She pulled the press. She bound the sheets.

This is not a tribute room. This is a Workshop. Here is the method I learn'd from Robert; here is the press at Hercules Buildings; here is the Prospectus I print'd to the Public in ninety-three.
