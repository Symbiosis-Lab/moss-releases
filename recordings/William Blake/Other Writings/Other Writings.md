Sub-sections:

- *french-revolution/* — Book I, 1791 (set in type at Joseph Johnson's, withheld from publication)
- *tiriel/* — manuscript poem, c. 1789, abandoned (text via Ellis-Yeats vol. II)
- *pickering-manuscript/* — fair-copy manuscript of ten short poems, c. 1807; six poems extracted, four pending
- *notebook/* — selections from the Rossetti Manuscript (BL Add. MS 49460):
  - *A Public Address* (c. 1810)
  - *A Vision of the Last Judgment* (c. 1810)
  - *The Everlasting Gospel* (c. 1818)
- *vala/* — *Vala, or The Four Zoas*, c. 1796–1807: Nights I and IX (text via Ellis-Yeats vol. III; interventionist text, caveat in front-matter)
- *notes-in-other-mens-books/* — *Annotations on Reynolds* (c. 1798–1809), 119k chars verbatim from Ellis-Yeats vol. II
