The proof title page reads:

> THE FRENCH REVOLUTION
> A POEM, IN SEVEN BOOKS.
> BOOK THE FIRST.
> LONDON: PRINTED FOR J. JOHNSON, No. 72, St. Paul's Church-yard.
> MDCCXCI. [PRICE ONE SHILLING.]

The proof contains the printer's "Advertisement" claiming "The remaining Books of this Poem are finished, and will be published in their Order" — but the remaining books are not known to have been completed.
