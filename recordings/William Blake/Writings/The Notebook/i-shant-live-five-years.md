A single line written by Blake in the Notebook (later known as the Rossetti Manuscript), datable to c. 1810 from its place among other entries of that period:

> I say I shan't live five years. And if I live one it will be a Wonder.

(Blake in fact lived another seventeen years from the probable date of writing, dying in August 1827.)

*Source:* Blake's Notebook, BL Add. MS 49460, c. 1810.
