**Complete — 5 of 5 plates** at ≈1220×1660 px each (Blake Archive, pass-9).

The work opens:

> O Times remote!
> When Love & Joy were adoration,
> And none impure were deem'd:
> Not Eyeless Covet,
> Nor Thin-lip'd Envy,
> Nor Bristled Wrath,
> Nor Curled Wantonness.

Los the eternal Prophet, in chains of the mind, beating the stubborn structure of his fallen Brother. The Hammer of Los strikes the Anvil of Urthona in the Vegetable World.
