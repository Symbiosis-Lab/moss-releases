Per Blake's pass-9 directive: *"After Marriage, Visions of the Daughters of Albion — short, fierce, often neglected."* Added the William Blake Archive Copy A as a coloured-companion view alongside the existing high-resolution LoC scans, and fixed the two carryover low-res slots in the root canonical-named set.

Sub-folders:
- *root files* `01-frontispiece.jpg`–`11-plate-11.jpg` — canonical plate-order at LoC Rosenwald resolution.
- *001.jpg–009.jpg* — parallel LoC scan view (sequential numbering, no per-plate captions).
- *copy-a-bma/* — Blake Archive Copy A (British Museum), object-order `01.jpg`–`11.jpg`. BA Copy A's binding places the frontispiece as object 11 (dbi `vda.a.p11-1`) and the title page as object 1 (dbi `vda.a.p1-2`).
