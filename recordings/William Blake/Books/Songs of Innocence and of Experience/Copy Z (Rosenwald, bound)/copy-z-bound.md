This is Copy Z as it sits at the Library of Congress, in the Rosenwald Collection. I bound the two halves of the Songs together late in life; this is one of the last copies I made, & it is fully coloured. The Library of Congress photographed it opening by opening — thirty-five spreads in all.

Read the book this way for the binding, the bookplates, the marbled endpapers — for the *physical fact* of the work, which is itself an inheritance from Lessing Rosenwald to the Nation.

Read it the other way — by individual plate, in the *innocence/* and *experience/* sub-rooms — for the design of each leaf.

(*The first three openings show the leather binding, the marbled endpapers with the Rosenwald gift bookplate, & the blank flyleaves. The body of the book begins at opening 4 with the general title-page facing the frontispiece. After that, each opening shows a printed plate on the verso of one leaf & a printed plate on the recto of the next — the openings of the bound volume.*)
