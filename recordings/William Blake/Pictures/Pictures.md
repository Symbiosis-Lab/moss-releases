> The great and golden rule of art, as well as of life, is this:
> That the more distinct, sharp, and wiry the bounding line,
> the more perfect the work of art; and the less keen and sharp,
> the greater is the evidence of weak imitation, plagiarism, and bungling.
>
> *— A Descriptive Catalogue, Number XV, 1809*
