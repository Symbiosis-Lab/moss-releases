In the year five Mr Robert Cromek the Publisher commissioned me to make twenty designs to Mr Robert Blair's poem *The Grave*. He paid me twenty guineas, with the understanding that I should engrave them myself.

He then took the engraving from me & gave it to Mr Louis Schiavonetti, who put a Continental polish upon the lines that emptied them of their meaning. Cromek published twelve of my designs (not the twenty I made) in a folio in eighteen-eight, & got himself praised for it. He paid me nothing more.

The designs are mine. The Engraving is not. The Reader will see Schiavonetti's smooth lines where mine would have been bony & particular.

The twelve published designs:

1. Frontispiece: *The Skeleton Re-Animated*
2. *Christ Descending into the Grave*
3. *The Meeting of a Family in Heaven*
4. *The Counsellor, King, Warrior, Mother, and Child in the Tomb*
5. *The Soul Hovering over the Body Reluctantly Parting with Life*
6. *The Death of the Strong Wicked Man*
7. *The Day of Judgment*
8. *The Reunion of the Soul and the Body*
9. *Death's Door*
10. *The Death of the Good Old Man*
11. *The Descent of Man into the Vale of Death*
12. *The Soul Exploring the Recesses of the Grave*
