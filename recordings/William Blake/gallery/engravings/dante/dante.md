Mr Linnell, after the Job, set me to Dante. I learnt enough Italian to read the *Commedia* in the original — I was sixty-eight years old when I began — & I made one hundred & two watercolor designs in the months that I had left.

Of these I engraved only seven before I died. Mr Linnell will see the remaining engravings through the press after me, & publish the watercolors as Folio in their own time.

The seven engravings:

1. *The Circle of the Lustful: Paolo and Francesca*
2. *Antaeus Setting Down Dante and Virgil in the Last Circle of Hell*
3. *The Devils with Dante and Virgil by the Side of the Pool*
4. *The Circle of the Thieves: Buoso Donati Attacked by the Serpent*
5. *The Six-Footed Serpent Attacking Agnelo Brunelleschi*
6. *The Circle of the Falsifiers: Dante and Virgil Covering Their Noses Because of the Stench*
7. *The Circle of the Traitors: Dante's Foot Striking Bocca degli Abati*

I do not love Dante as I love Milton. He has built Hell as a System, & I think a Hell built into a System is a worse Hell than one left to its own confusion. But the Vision is great, & the matter is great.
