Mr Linnell, my friend & patron in my latter years, commission'd these of me in the year twenty-one. He paid me a pound a week against the work for five years, & advanced money when I needed it, which was often.

I engraved twenty-one designs after my own watercolours of the same subjects (which I painted twice — once for Mr Butts, c. 1805, & again for Mr Linnell, c. 1821, with slight differences). The title-plate I composed last. The whole was published by Linnell in March of twenty-six, by Subscription. Mr Linnell sold the impressions for three guineas a set; he gave me half. It is the only Engraved Work of mine that was bought, in numbers, in my lifetime.

I cut the borders deep so that the Reader sees not one Picture but a Picture with its margins thinking out loud. Each plate carries Scripture on the engraved frame — text from the Book of Job, & sometimes from Ecclesiastes & Isaiah & the New Testament — that the Reader may know I am not making a new Bible but reading the old one.
