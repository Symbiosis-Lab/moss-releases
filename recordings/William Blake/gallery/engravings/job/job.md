**The wheel is built and the hub is in.**

Blake's commentary (per his pass-4 review): *"In plate 1 Job kneels with his harps hung in the willow; in plate 14 the harps come down & the sons of God shout for joy. Without Morning Stars the cycle does not turn, it only arranges itself."*

The 21 Met plates are at archival resolution (~2800×3600 px). Morning Stars (plate 14) is currently at 1571×1920 px — about 30% of the linear resolution and 9% of the pixel area of its neighbors. Sufficient for reading; visibly softer in a grid view. Future passes should re-acquire plate 14 directly from Yale Center for British Art's IIIF endpoint for B1978.43.1516 to bring the entire series to parity.
