**The wheel is built and the hub is in.**

Blake's commentary (per his pass-4 review): *"In plate 1 Job kneels with his harps hung in the willow; in plate 14 the harps come down & the sons of God shout for joy. Without Morning Stars the cycle does not turn, it only arranges itself."*

The 21 Met plates are at archival resolution (~2800×3600 px). Morning Stars (plate 14) is now at 1920×2347 px (pass-7 upgrade — Yale via Wikimedia 1920px Varnish-cached thumbnail, since direct YCBA IIIF + the original-resolution `upload.wikimedia.org` URL are both blocked by Cloudflare/rate-limit). About 68% of the linear resolution of its Met neighbours and 43% of the pixel area — still moderately soft in a side-by-side grid view, but legible at typical display sizes. A future pass with YCBA research-access credentials should re-acquire plate 14 at the full ~3200×3900 archival resolution.
