A great copper-plate engraving, three feet wide, after my own tempera painting of the Pilgrimage to Canterbury. I cut every line myself. It is the largest engraving I ever made, & I sold it by Subscription with my own Prospectus, that the Public might judge it against Mr Stothard's version which Mr Cromek had commissioned in plain theft of my design.

The Subscribers were few but they were true. Mr Butts subscribed, Mr Cumberland subscribed, Mr Linnell subscribed (it was through this plate he first knew of me). The Stothard print, engraved by Schiavonetti & published by Cromek with a great fanfare, was puffed in all the journals; mine was barely noticed. But mine is the True picture, & I am content for Posterity to judge.

I wrote a Public Address on this work that I have kept in my Notebook — see Other Writings.
