Mr Richard Edwards the bookseller commissioned me in the year ninety-five to make designs for an edition of Dr Edward Young's *Night Thoughts*. I drew five hundred & thirty-seven watercolours upon large folio paper, around the printed text — every page surrounded by Vision.

Of these, Mr Edwards engraved forty-three for the first part of the edition (Nights I to IV) & published them as Folio in ninety-seven. The remaining Nights were never published. The Public did not buy what was issued; the book was a costly Folio at five guineas a copy, & most copies sat in the booksellers' rooms unsold.

After this venture, the Trade considered me Unsaleable. Mr Edwards lost his money & withdrew. The remaining five hundred & thirty-four watercolours went into Mr Butts's collection.

I do not regret the work. The designs of Night IV — the contention of Death with the Christian — are as good as anything I have made.
