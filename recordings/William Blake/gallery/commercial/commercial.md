The trade I was apprenticed to. Engraving under commission paid the rent at Lambeth & at South Molton Street, & it was the only trade I had until Mr Linnell's commissions in my last years. Some of this work I am proud of; some I did because we needed bread.

The chief commercial commissions, all publicly issued:

## Stedman's Narrative of a Five Years' Expedition (1796)

Sixteen plates engraved for Captain John Gabriel Stedman's *Narrative*, published by Joseph Johnson. Captain Stedman had served in Surinam against the revolted negroes & had come back with terrible drawings of the cruelties he saw practised by the planters upon the slaves. He gave me the drawings & I engraved them. The plates carry his witness — & where they were too plain for the public eye, that is the very point.

Subjects include:
- *A Negro Hung Alive by the Ribs to a Gallows*
- *The Execution of Breaking on the Rack*
- *Flagellation of a Female Samboe Slave*
- *Group of Negros, as imported to be sold for Slaves*
- *March thro' a swamp or Marsh in Terra-firma*
- *Family of Negro Slaves from Loango*

I engraved them in eighteen sheets between ninety-three & ninety-six. The Reader will see Stedman's witness through my burin; the cruelty is his account, the lines are mine. Many copies of the book sold; Mr Wilberforce was a Subscriber. The trade in slaves was abolished in the year seven, in part by such witnesses as these.

## Hesiod, after Flaxman (1817)

Thirty-seven plates engraved after Mr Flaxman's outline drawings, published by Mr Longman in 1817. Flaxman's outline style is simple, & my burin followed it patiently. A handsome folio.

## Hayley's *Designs to a Series of Ballads* (1802) & *Life of Romney* (1809)

Plates engraved at Felpham & after for Mr Hayley's books. Four ballad plates in two; six for the Romney biography. Pleasant patron-work that paid the rent.

## Cumberland's *Thoughts on Outline* (1796)

Eight plates after Mr George Cumberland's antique designs. The first of my dealings with Cumberland, who became a friend for life.

## Wollstonecraft's *Original Stories from Real Life* (1791)

Six plates for Mary Wollstonecraft's children's book, published by Mr Johnson. Mrs Wollstonecraft was sharp-spoken & did not flatter. I respected her.
