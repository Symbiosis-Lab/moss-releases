The twelve canonical subjects of Blake's 1795 Large Color Prints:

1. **Newton** (Tate)  ✓
2. **Nebuchadnezzar** (Tate)  ✓
3. **Pity** (Tate)  ✓
4. **Hecate, or The Night of Enitharmon's Joy** (Tate)  ✓
5. **Elohim Creating Adam** (Tate)  ✓
6. **Satan Exulting over Eve** (Getty 84.GG.39)  ✓
7. **God Judging Adam** (Tate, re-identified by Butlin 1965) — *pending*
8. **Lamech and His Two Wives** (Tate)  ✓
9. **Naomi entreating Ruth and Orpah** (V&A)  ✓
10. **Christ Appearing to the Apostles** (NGA Washington) — *pending*
11. **The House of Death** (Tate)  ✓
12. **The Good and Evil Angels** (Tate)  ✓

Ten of twelve subjects present. The two pending plates (God Judging Adam, Christ Appearing) were rate-limited on Wikimedia at acquisition time; both are public-domain and acquirable from Wikimedia or directly from the holding institutions (Tate Images / NGA Washington).
