Here are the public Designs that were not illuminated Books. The Job & the Dante I engraved late, for Mr Linnell. The Large Color Prints I made in ninety-five at Hercules Buildings, by a method of my own. The Canterbury Pilgrims I painted & then engraved myself, against Mr Stothard who had been my friend & against Mr Cromek who paid me poorly. The Temperas are my Fresco — not in the Italian manner, but in mine. The 1809 Exhibition was held in my brother's house at 28 Broad Street, & few came.

The Refusals are a small room. A Reader who sees only what I made will not understand what I would not make.
