description: Tempera paintings. Blake called the medium 'Fresco': watercolour on a ground prepared with carpenter's glue. Examples include the Heads of the Poets (Manchester), *Bathsheba* (Tate), *Adam Naming the Beasts* and *Eve Naming the Birds* (Glasgow Museums), *Christ in the Sepulchre, Guarded by Angels* (V&A), *Spiritual Form of Pitt/Nelson* (Tate, exhibited 1809), *Satan Calling Up His Legions* (V&A), *The Ghost of a Flea* (Tate).
children_style: grid
source: Holdings dispersed across UK institutions; verbatim Blake commentary in his 1809 *Descriptive Catalogue* for the works exhibited that year (Pitt, Nelson, Canterbury Pilgrims, Bard from Gray, etc.). Public domain.
