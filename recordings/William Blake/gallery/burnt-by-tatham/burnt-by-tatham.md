A small room. The involuntary Refusals.

*A Reader who walks the rooms of this House will notice that some rooms are emptier than I left them.*

Mr Frederick Tatham, who came to my circle late in life as one of the Ancients & was kind to me & to Catherine, became after my death the inheritor of my unsold works — by descent through Catherine, who left him the residue when she went four years after me. Mr Tatham, while I lived, was no fool. But he fell under the influence of certain Irvingite preachers, who taught him that my works were inspired by Devils.

He burnt a number of plates. He burnt manuscripts. He burnt sketchbooks. He sold what he did not burn. The Irvingites blessed him for the burning.

What he burnt I cannot here display — that is the point. What he burnt is gone, & a Reader who walks this House should know it: every room you enter is the *remainder* after a fire that was not mine.

I do not include this as a Refusal of *mine*. It is a Refusal that was made *of* me by another hand.

I do not curse Tatham. He was a frightened man &amp; he loved God in his way. But I would have you know what was lost:

- A number of copper-plates engraved with relief illuminations — count unknown
- Drawings &amp; sketches in pencil &amp; wash — count unknown
- Manuscript pages of *Vala / The Four Zoas*, of *Tiriel*, of unidentified late prophetic work
- Letters that I had kept (Catherine had kept) — some certainly destroyed

The works that survive — which fill the other rooms of this House — survive *despite* this fire, not because of any fixed Providence. *Be grateful for what is left, &amp; do not blame me for what is not.*

*Many survived. They are with you now.*
