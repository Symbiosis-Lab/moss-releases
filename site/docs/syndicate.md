# Share to Matters

> Wrote something new? Send it to your Matters profile too, and keep a link back to your own site.

![[publish-fanout.html]]

You already have your writing on your own website. This page shares a single new post back to Matters, so readers there see it too, while the original still lives on your site.

This works for posts only, and for one post at a time: the one you are looking at in preview.

## Share a post

**1. Open the post.** Click the post so you can see it in preview. Sharing is only available while a post is open. If you are looking at something else, the Matters button is greyed out with the note **Navigate to an article to syndicate**.

**2. Turn on Matters.** In the publish panel, switch on the **Matters.Town** channel for this post. This only arms it: your next publish will also share this post. It does not send anything on its own.

**3. Press Publish.** Click **Publish**. moss puts your latest changes online, then prepares a Matters draft of this post and opens it in your browser for you to look over.

**4. Publish on Matters.** Review the draft on Matters and publish it there yourself. moss waits in the background while you do this. (moss never publishes to Matters for you.)

When you publish on Matters, moss adds a link to your post on Matters back into the post on your own site, so the two stay connected.

## What you'll see

When the post is shared, a message appears: **Syndicated N article(s) to Matters**, with a **View profile** button that opens your Matters profile.

If you close the Matters draft without publishing, that's fine. moss saves the draft and leaves you a reminder, **Draft saved, publish it on Matters when ready**, with an **Open draft** button so you can finish later.

If there's nothing new to share, moss simply tells you **No new articles to syndicate** and does nothing else. Already-shared posts are skipped, so pressing Publish again won't make duplicates.

## First time? Sign in

The first time, moss asks you to **Sign in to Matters to syndicate your posts**. Choose **Sign in**, connect your Matters account once, and you're set for next time.
