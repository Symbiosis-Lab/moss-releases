# Import from Matters

> Already write on Matters? Bring your posts in, and moss turns them into a website that's yours.

Your writing on Matters becomes your own website. You add Matters once, log in, and moss copies your posts over: cover images, the pictures inside, even how many people commented and liked. Then it turns them into your site, ready to put online. Nothing on Matters changes.

## Add Matters

**1. Open the channel list.** In the top-right of the main window is a small **+** button. Hover it and you'll see **Add channel**. Click it. moss opens settings on the **Channels** tab, where the places you can share your writing live.

**2. Add Matters.** Find the **Matters** icon. Hover it and a hint reads **Matters (click to add)**. Click it. The hint flips to **Matters (click to remove)**, which tells you it's added. (Matters isn't there until you add it.)

## Log in

Right after you add Matters, moss opens the real Matters login page in a small window. Log in the way you always do.

Then comes the one surprising part: after you log in, the window sits still for about twenty seconds and does nothing. **This is normal.** moss waits, then checks for you, and as soon as it's in, it closes the window by itself. You don't have to click anything.

## Your posts come in

moss starts copying your posts, and a slim progress line appears near your writing to show how it's going. There's no bar at the bottom of the window; watch the progress near the title instead.

As it works, you'll see it move through your writing: **Fetching articles from Matters.town...**, then **Found N article(s) to sync**, then each post in turn as **Syncing article: {title}**. The longest stretch is the pictures, shown as **Downloading n/m...**. Your posts arrive one by one, each with its cover image and the pictures inside, plus how many comments and likes it had.

Your posts land in a folder named **文章** (Articles) if you write on Matters in Chinese, or **articles** otherwise.

A few things to know:

- The first time takes a while, because moss brings in everything. After that it only checks for what's new or changed, so later runs are quick.
- Drafts stay on Matters. moss brings in your published posts, not your unfinished ones.

## Your site comes together

As your posts arrive, moss turns them into a website and shows it in the right-hand pane. This preview is just for you. Nothing is online yet, so look around and read it over.

## Put it online

**1. Press Publish.** In the top-right is a round button. Hover it and you'll see **Publish**. Click it. moss opens a short setup, titled **Set up your site**.

**2. Pick your web address and enter your email.** moss suggests an address ending in `.mosspub.com`, like `my-blog`, that you can keep or edit. It checks it and shows **Available** or **Not available**. Type your email under **Email**, then choose **Verify email**.

**3. Click the link in your email.** moss shows **Check your email** and waits, with **Waiting for verification...** Open the email it sent and click the link. moss notices and moves on by itself. (If you've published with this email before, moss skips this and the button simply reads **Publish**.) If the email doesn't arrive, use **Resend**, or **Change email** to try another.

**4. You're live.** moss shows **Setting up your site...**, then **Published.** with the line **Your site is live** and your web address. Click **Visit site** to see it, or **Later** to keep writing.

That's the whole thing. Your Matters writing is now a website that's yours. Any time you change a post or want your newest Matters posts, press **Publish** again and your site updates.
