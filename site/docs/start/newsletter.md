# Send a newsletter

> Already have readers? Email your latest post straight to their inbox, from your own site.

Your writing is on your website. A newsletter lets you send a post to people by email too, so readers hear from you directly. You turn the newsletter on once, add the people who want it, then send any post you like.

Newsletter sending works for sites hosted on moss. If your site lives somewhere else, there's no mailing to send from yet.

## Turn on the newsletter

**1. Open Channels.** In settings, open the **Channels** tab. This is where the ways you share your writing live.

**2. Add the newsletter.** Find the **Email Newsletter** tile, described as **Send articles to email subscribers**. Click the small **+** badge on its icon to switch it on. The badge flips to a **−**, which tells you it's on. That's all it takes.

## Add your readers

**1. Open Subscribers.** In settings, open the **Subscribers** tab. You'll see a **Subscribers** heading with a small count next to it, and a list of everyone signed up.

**2. Press + Add.** Click **+ Add** to open a short form.

**3. Type the emails.** Under **Emails (one per line)**, paste or type one address per line. If your site has more than one language, you'll pick a **Language** first, so each reader hears from you in the right one. A note reminds you these are **Added without confirmation email.**, so only add people who asked to hear from you.

**4. Press Add.** Click **Add**. The form closes, the list refreshes, and the count next to **Subscribers** goes up. If an address can't be added, moss tells you right there and adds nothing.

Each reader has a status, shown as a small mark you can hover for the word: **Confirmed**, **Unverified**, **Pending**, **Bounced**, **Complained**, or **Unsubscribed**. To remove someone, click the **×** at the end of their row. To keep a copy of your list, use **Export CSV** at the bottom.

## Send a post

**1. Turn on email for the post.** Open the post you want to send. In the publish panel, switch on the newsletter channel for this post. This only arms it: nothing is sent yet. Email is off by default, so each post is your website only until you say otherwise.

**2. Press Publish.** moss puts your latest changes online first. Once your site updates, the email composer opens on the left so you can write the message.

**3. Fill in the message.** The composer shows a few rows:

- **From**: your name, sending from your site's address at `your-site@mosspub.com`. If you've set your email in settings, a line confirms replies reach you.
- **To**: the readers who'll get this. On a site with more than one language, pick which group.
- **Subject**: the line readers see first. Type one under **Enter a subject**.
- **Preview**: an optional **Short hook shown below the subject line in inboxes**.

The body starts from your post and you can edit it freely. Your changes are saved as a draft and never touch the published post.

**4. Send a test first.** Click **Send test** to mail a copy to yourself, so you can see exactly how it lands. (Set your email in settings to use this.)

**5. Send it.** Click **Send → N**, where N is how many readers will get it. moss shows a recap, **Send to N subscribers?**, so you can check the From, To, Subject, and Preview one last time. Press **Send now → N** to send, or **Go back** to keep editing.

## What you'll see

When it's on its way, a message confirms the send and the composer closes. Your draft is cleared, because it's done its job. Your readers now have your post in their inbox, and your site stays exactly as it is.
