# Give Your Site an Address

> Want your site at your own name, like yourname.com? Find one, buy it, and connect it without leaving moss.

Your site already lives online. This page gives it your own address, so people reach it at a name you choose instead of a longer default one. moss helps you find a name, buy it, and set it up for you.

You need a moss subscription to buy an address. If you don't have one yet, moss will let you know when you try.

## Find a name

**1. Open settings.** In settings, open the **Deployment** tab. You'll see a **Domain** row.

**2. Search.** Click the **Domain** box (it reads **Search for a domain**) and type the name you want. A list opens, grouped into **YOUR DOMAINS**, **EXACT MATCH**, **AVAILABLE TO PURCHASE**, and **TAKEN**. Names you can buy show a price. Names someone already owns are marked **taken**.

**3. Pick one.** Click a name that's available. A short checkout opens right below it.

If you've already bought an address through moss before, the footer link **Already purchased a domain? Verify your email to see them.** brings it back. Verify your email first, then it appears under **YOUR DOMAINS**.

## Buy it

You'll need a verified email. If you haven't verified yet, moss shows **Please verify your email above before purchasing a domain.** Verify your email in the row just above, then come back.

**1. Fill in your details.** Enter **Country**, **Name**, **Phone**, and **Street address**. Outside the US you'll also enter a **City**. moss explains why: **ICANN requires accurate contact info to keep your domain active. We use Contact Privacy to keep it out of public WHOIS.** Your details stay private, not public.

**2. Add a card.** Enter your card. moss notes: **Your card will be saved for future renewals and purchases. You can change it anytime.**

**3. Buy.** Click the button, which reads **Buy for** the price. It shows **Processing...** while it works. To stop, click **Cancel**.

## Watch it come online

After you buy, moss sets everything up for you and shows a panel under the **Domain** box. At the top it reads **Setting up HOST · usually ready in a few minutes** (HOST is your new address). Below it, a few steps move from waiting to done:

- **Domain registration**
- **Public DNS for HOST**
- **Certificate for HOST**
- **Site reachable at HOST**

This usually takes a few minutes. moss keeps checking on its own. When everything is ready, the panel reads **HOST is live** and a **Visit HOST** button appears. Click it to open your site at its new address.

If it's slow, moss may say **Taking longer than usual** and keep trying. It also notes: **If your network shows an old version, it may take a few minutes to update.** If something doesn't finish, you'll see **Cannot reach HOST**, with **Recheck** to try again or **Dismiss** to close the panel. If your connection drops, moss shows **Your network seems offline. We'll keep trying once it's back.** and picks up on its own.

Once your site is live at the new address, the **Domain** row shows **Deployed** with how long ago, and can note that your old address still redirects there, so older links keep working.
