If you already publish on Matters, create an empty folder and name it after your site. Right-click it and choose **Publish**, then pick **Plugins** → **Matters** in the editor's guide card. Once you log in, the Matters plugin downloads your articles and collections into the folder, and moss shows a live preview of your site as it goes.

%% Empty folder, right-click Publish -> install Matters -> preview %%
![[folder-to-site.html]]

You can also install the Matters plugin on an existing site. Click the "+" to install it, and Matters appears among your publish channels. When you finish a post, tick the Matters channel and click Publish. After moss deploys the site, it opens the Matters editor so you can confirm.
